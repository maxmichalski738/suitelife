Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Data Controller

Sortd is operated by Max Michalski as a sole trader. For privacy questions, contact: sortd.help@proton.me

Data Stored On Your Device

Your entries, document photos, scan history and app settings are stored only on your device, in files protected by iOS file-level encryption. Sortd does not operate any servers or databases that hold your personal data. Your data is included in your device's iCloud backup if you have iCloud Backup turned on in iOS Settings.

Passwords & Logins

Entries in the Passwords & Logins section are stored in the iOS Keychain — Apple's secure credential storage, the same system used by Safari and every other app that saves passwords. Keychain entries are hardware-encrypted and isolated from the rest of the app's data. They are never included in data exports and are never sent to any external service, including Anthropic.

AI Document Scanning

When you scan or import a document, on-device OCR extracts text from the image. That extracted text (never the image itself) is sent to Anthropic's API to identify the document type, key dates, and details. This processing is necessary to provide the scanning feature. Anthropic acts as our data processor and does not use API data for model training. API operational logs are deleted after 30 days.

Anthropic is based in the United States. We rely on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) as the mechanism for transferring personal data from the UK to the US.

AI Chat (Sortd AI)

When you send a message in the AI chat tab, your message and a summary of your saved entries are sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response. The summary includes the name, section, dates, and notes from your entries.

Entries you have marked as Private, all Health section entries, and all Passwords & Logins entries are always excluded and are never sent to any external service.

Home Screen AI Insight

Once per day, Sortd may generate a proactive insight about your entries — for example, flagging something that's about to expire. This sends a brief summary of your entries to Anthropic's API to generate the suggestion. The same exclusions apply: Private, Health, and Passwords & Logins entries are never included. You can dismiss these suggestions and they will not reappear until the following day.

Feedback

If you submit feedback using the Send Feedback form, your message is sent to Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party provider based in the United States. We rely on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) as the mechanism for transferring personal data from the UK to the US for this purpose. Please do not include passwords or sensitive personal details in feedback messages.

Notifications

Sortd can send local notifications to remind you about upcoming dates. These are generated entirely on your device and are not processed by any external server.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not collect analytics or usage data.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not require an account to use the app.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data.
  • We do not have access to your entries, notes, photos, or Passwords & Logins.

In-App Purchases

Sortd Pro is available as a monthly subscription (£1.99/month), annual subscription (£9.99/year), or one-time lifetime purchase (£29.99). All transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store under Apple's privacy policy. We receive only confirmation that a purchase was made — we never receive payment details, your name, or any financial information.

Lawful Basis for Processing (UK GDPR)

AI scanning, AI chat, and the home screen AI insight: performance of the contract between you and Sortd (you have requested these features). Device storage: necessary to provide the app. We do not process your data for marketing, profiling, or advertising.

Special Category Data

If you store health information (prescriptions, blood type, NHS number, etc.) in the Health section, this is special category data under UK GDPR Article 9. We process it on the basis of your explicit consent, granted by choosing to store it. Health section entries are never sent to any third party.

Retention

Your data remains on your device until you delete individual entries or use "Delete all data" in Settings. Deleting the app permanently removes all locally stored data. Anthropic deletes API logs after 30 days.

Your Rights Under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access your data — use Export in Settings
  • Correct or delete your data — edit or delete entries, or use "Delete all data" in Settings
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability — use Export in Settings

Contact us at sortd.help@proton.me to exercise any right.

Right to Complain

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.

Children's Privacy

Sortd is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. All data is stored locally on the device.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Sortd after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

For privacy questions or to exercise your data subject rights: sortd.help@proton.me
