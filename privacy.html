Privacy Policy Sortd  ·  Last updated: June 2025 Sortd is designed from the ground up to keep your personal information private. Your data lives on your device. We don't run servers that store your entries, we don't sell your data, and we don't show you ads. What information Sortd stores Sortd stores everything you add — entries, dates, notes, and photos — locally on your device. This data never passes through our servers. It is saved to your iPhone's private app storage and is not accessible to other apps. If you enable iCloud sync (a Pro feature), your data is synced through your personal Apple iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit infrastructure. This is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We never have access to this data. The AI document scanner When you scan a document, here is exactly what happens: • Your camera captures an image. That image never leaves your device. • Apple's on-device Vision framework reads the text from the image. This happens entirely on your iPhone, with no network connection. • The extracted text (not the image) is sent to the Anthropic API, which identifies what kind of document it is and suggests how to file it. • The result is returned to your app. The text is not stored or used for training by Anthropic. Anthropic's API terms explicitly state that data submitted via the API is not used to train their models. You can read their policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy. Family sharing If you use Sortd's family sharing feature (Pro), your entries are synced with your household via Apple CloudKit shared containers. Family members must be invited and must accept the invitation before they can see any shared entries. When creating or editing any entry, you can mark it as Private. Private entries are stored only on your device and are never included in family sync — not even when family mode is turned on. The lock icon on an entry means only you can see it. We do not operate a family-sharing server. All sync runs through Apple's infrastructure under your Apple ID. Notifications Sortd can send you local notifications to remind you about upcoming dates and renewals. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and are never sent to or processed by an external server. You can turn notifications off at any time in your iPhone's Settings app. What we do not collect • We do not collect analytics or usage data. • We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs of any kind. • We do not require you to create an account to use the app. • We do not have access to your entries, notes, photos, or scanned documents. • We do not sell, share, or monetise your personal information in any way. In-app purchases Sortd offers a one-time Pro upgrade purchase. This transaction is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, but we do not receive your payment details, name, or any financial information. This is handled entirely by Apple under their own privacy policy. Children's privacy Sortd is rated 4+ and contains no content inappropriate for children. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone. Because all data is stored locally on the device, there is no data collection that would affect children differently from any other user. Data deletion You can delete all of your Sortd data at any time by going to Settings within the app and tapping "Delete account & all data". This removes everything from your device. If you have iCloud sync enabled, you can also delete the iCloud data from your iPhone's Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage. Uninstalling the app from your iPhone will also remove all locally stored data. Third-party services Sortd uses two third-party services: • Anthropic Claude API — used only during document scanning to classify extracted text. Anthropic Privacy Policy • Apple CloudKit / iCloud — used for optional sync and family sharing.
