Frequently Asked Questions

How does document scanning work?

Tap the + button and choose Scan. Point your camera at any document — insurance certificate, passport, bill, prescription — and Sortd reads it and files it automatically. For best results, make sure the document is well-lit and fully in frame.

Is my data stored online?

No. Everything you add to Sortd is stored only on your device. Sortd does not run any servers or databases that hold your personal data. Your data is included in your device's iCloud backup if you have iCloud Backup enabled in iOS Settings.

How are my Passwords & Logins kept safe?

Passwords & Logins entries are stored in the iOS Keychain — the same secure, hardware-encrypted storage used by Safari. They are protected by Face ID and never exported or sent to any external service.

What does the AI actually send to the internet?

When you scan a document, only the extracted text is sent to Anthropic's API — never the photo. When you use AI chat, your message and a brief summary of your entries are sent. Health, Private, and Passwords & Logins entries are always excluded. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I set up expiry reminders?

When adding or editing an entry, set a date in the Date field. Sortd will automatically remind you 30 days, 7 days, and 1 day before. Make sure notifications are enabled for Sortd in your iPhone's Settings app.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you've previously purchased Sortd Pro, go to Settings → Upgrade → Restore Purchases. Your subscription or lifetime access will be restored automatically. If you're signed in to the same Apple ID used to purchase, it will be recognised straight away.

Can I use Sortd on multiple devices?

Sortd stores data locally, so entries added on one device don't automatically appear on another. A Sortd Pro subscription is tied to your Apple ID and works on any device signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings → Delete All Data. This permanently removes all entries, photos, and scan history from your device. This cannot be undone.
